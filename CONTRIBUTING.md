# Contributing

Reproducible counterexamples, source-specific corrections and clearer limitations are welcome through this repository's issues or pull requests. Identify the study, release, claim ID, exact input and expected/observed behavior. State whether an example is original, reconstructed or newly invented, and disclose relevant dependencies.

Submit only material you may share publicly. Do not attach private application source, personal records, credentials, sealed evaluation answers or restricted reviewer submissions. Contact the maintainer through the [public profile](https://github.com/eranb007) before proposing restricted material. The file-specific licenses are listed in [LICENSES/scope.json](LICENSES/scope.json): MIT for eligible original scripts and CC BY 4.0 for eligible original text and chart expression. Data and metadata terms are described in [LICENSES/DATA-TERMS.md](LICENSES/DATA-TERMS.md). Contributions require an explicit rights and disclosure review and agreement on applicable terms before inclusion. No contributor license agreement is in place, and a submission does not automatically grant rights in third-party or uncertain material. No contribution automatically changes frozen historical results.
