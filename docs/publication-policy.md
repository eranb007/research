# Publication policy

The workflow distinguishes private evidence, candidate preparation, review, owner approval and actual publication. Each is a separate event.

Private evidence stays in its protected environment. A public package is an explicit selection of checked representations in separate staging. Its private provenance map and approval receipts are never part of the public payload. A public evidence ID or hash identifies provenance without implying source access.

Scientific, structural, disclosure, rights and reproduction checks declare their scope. Passing a validator is neither owner approval nor legal certification. Owner approval binds an exact file manifest, identity, destination and permitted actions. Any changed file, claim, license or disclosure requires review of a new identity.

Reader-facing documents describe the content they accompany. Preparation decisions and rejected candidates remain accurately dated in the private audit, rather than masquerading as the current public state. A content manifest establishes integrity; it is not a publication receipt. Actual publication is established separately by a verified commit, published-file hashes, time and destination URL. Never infer publication from draft existence or a proposed version.

Publish at meaningful scientific milestones, methodology corrections, reviewed articles or significant negative results. Do not mirror private commits or transfer private history. Preserve earlier releases; corrections receive explicit change notes. No publishing automation or default-branch change is enabled by these procedures.
