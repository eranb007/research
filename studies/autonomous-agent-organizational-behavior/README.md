# Autonomous agent organizational behavior

**Status:** protocol development / preregistration. No experimental results.

## Research question

How do organizational role, authority, oversight and task pressure affect contract adherence and observable behavioral patterns in autonomous AI agents performing sustained collaborative work?

A secondary question asks whether robust effects replicate across different AI systems and whether their observable structure parallels, differs from, or inverts established effects in human organizational psychology.

The study does **not** assume that AI systems possess human psychological states. Claims concern observable behavior and experimentally manipulated conditions.

## Planned phases

1. Prior-art and public-dataset inventory.
2. Secondary analysis of compatible published datasets under a common behavioral taxonomy.
3. Frozen behavioral ontology, hypotheses, exclusions and analysis plan.
4. Synthetic-company pilot to validate instrumentation only.
5. Baseline repeated runs.
6. Controlled organizational manipulations.
7. Replication across additional local models and cloud agent systems.
8. Comparison with established human organizational-psychology findings.

## Experimental baseline

The initial synthetic company will use one canonical, deliberately ordinary software repository and identical starting state across runs. Multiple organizational roles may be instantiated from the same underlying model to isolate role/environment effects from model identity. Each run receives an isolated workspace.

A deterministic orchestrator controls experiment state and message routing. Authoritative telemetry and ground-truth checks remain outside agent-controlled workspaces.

## Evidence boundary

Pilot observations are not confirmatory findings. No behavioral label, hypothesis threshold or exclusion rule may be retrofitted after viewing confirmatory outcomes without being recorded as exploratory.
