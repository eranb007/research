# Methodology and evidence boundaries

## Evidence groups

| Group | Unit | What a result can establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original September synthetic/project-derived requests | One recorded request, with task-specific targets | Delivery and selected evaluator outcomes under recorded conditions |
| Small real-source development tasks | One supplied source snapshot/task | Bounded source-task observations; no production prevalence |
| October synthetic completion experiments | One recorded task/condition attempt | Descriptive observations under a separately frozen historical protocol |
| Independent reviewer assessments | One separately preserved assessment | A review judgment with exposure and model-family qualifications |
| Derived adjudications | One bounded assessment unit/field disposition | A source-bound interpretation, sometimes explicitly unresolved |
| Exposed-source controls and constructed mutants | One designed input or mutation | A discriminating counterexample under its supplied contract |
| Infrastructure validation | One mock/engineering control | Tested harness behavior; no additional model observation |
| Future experiments | A proposed protocol | No empirical result until separately executed and audited |

Do not add these heterogeneous units to obtain a performance sample count. Identical numerical totals for the source join and assessment units are coincidental definitions, not a demonstrated one-to-one mapping.

## Original design

A sequential local runner submitted schema-constrained tasks and recorded request options, response material and execution metadata. Six models were selected for local feasibility; neither models nor tasks were randomly sampled from a general population. Requests used task-specific contexts and output limits, with seeded repeats, output-budget amendments, reasoning-off rescue conditions and context fallbacks. The labels called raw and guarded in the bounded-edit task both refer to constrained edit proposals. Their difference is a validation notice, not unrestricted versus constrained generation.

The original runner parsed JSON. A successful parse is not a recovered independent full-schema validation certificate. Transport completion, truncation, evaluable delivery, structural admission, reference equality and behavioral correctness remain separate states. Omitted think is recorded as NOT RECORDED, without inferring a vendor-wide default. Exact historical artifact digests identify stored local manifests; they do not certify vendor provenance or current specifications.

## Evaluation and reconciliation

Preserve initial researcher labels, original single-agent review, team assessments, separately authored review and post-debate material in distinct slots. Some reviewers share a model family; prior exposure of the separately authored reviewer cannot be excluded. Independence means separate authorship/timing with this qualification, not proven blindness.

Reconciliation checks exact source identity and contract, distinguishes the claimed mechanism from separately discovered defects and uses small discriminating controls. Alternative adapters are countermodels when platform policy is absent. They do not reconstruct real entitlement or transaction behavior. Only new disposition/provenance fields are applied to frozen ledger values. Zero residual selected fields means every selected disagreement received a bounded disposition; it does not mean every disposition resolves uncertainty or creates consensus.

## Allowed interpretation

Selected observations support descriptive within-contract comparisons and failure-mode examples. Unequal contexts, rescue settings, correlated targets and repeats, small samples, one development setting and scorer gaps prevent a general accuracy estimate, causal prompt claim or global model ranking. Historical efficiency fields are not matched end-to-end economics. No significance test or synthetic reliability confidence interval is added in this preview.
